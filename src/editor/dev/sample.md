# Kyber Crystal Notes

Crystals are gathered on [[Ilum]] and attuned through long meditation. Cross-reference the [[Lightsaber Construction|construction checklist]] before the next expedition — **never** attune two crystals in one session. See the [archive index](https://example.com/archives) and use `attune --slow` for *stubborn* crystals. ~~Old method~~ retired.

![[crystal.png|180]]

## Attunement

1. Clear the mind; let the crystal choose its hue.
2. Record the resonance frequency in the [[Crystal Log]].
3. Seal the crystal in the hilt only after it holds steady for a full cycle.

> [!note]
> Every crystal resonates differently. A red shift means the crystal was bled — log it and set it aside.

> [!warning] Handle with care
> Never expose a crystal to an unshielded reactor core.
